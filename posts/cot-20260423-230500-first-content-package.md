## PRODUCTION PLAN: 3-SHOT CHAIN

### Shot 1 — HIS WORLD (15s)

**Seedance 2.0 / Kling 3.0 Prompt:**
```
Hyper-realistic cinematic, 35mm film grain, warm amber tones. 
A man lies in bed at night, face lit by phone glow — blue-white light against warm dark room. 
He scrolls slowly, jaw slightly tense, eyes scanning — a blueprint appears on screen, then code, then a map, then a face he doesn't know. 
His expression: focused, searching, a faint frown, then a slow exhale through his nose. 
His hand rests on his chest, fingers slightly curled. 
He glances sideways — off screen left, where she would be — then back to the phone. 
Camera: slow push-in from wide shot to medium close-up, shallow depth of field, warm bedside lamp glow behind him, his face half in shadow.
Candlelight warmth on walls. Slight lens flare from lamp.
VHS warmth overlay, slightly overexposed highlights, handheld intimacy.
```

**Object anchor:** His hand on his chest — establish here, callback in Shot 3

**Cost/obstacle:** He glances toward her but doesn't reach over. Not yet.

---

### Shot 2 — HER WORLD (15s)

**Seedance 2.0 / Kling 3.0 Prompt:**
```
Continuing directly from previous shot, same warm bedroom, same candlelight walls, same night.
A woman lies in bed, same side, phone-lit face — but warmer screen glow, soft pink-gold tones.
She scrolls slowly: a flower arrangement, a friend's baby photo, a sunset someone shared, a poem.
Her expression: soft open eyes, a small smile that forms and fades, lips part slightly, she exhales.
Her free hand traces the edge of the duvet absently, fingers moving like she's thinking.
She glances sideways — off screen right, where he would be — a longer look this time. 
She doesn't move. Just watches that direction for a moment.
Camera: mirror of Shot 1 — slow push-in from wide to medium close-up, same warm depth of field.
Her face warm, alive, slightly lonely.
35mm film grain, soft vignette edges, warm amber and rose tones.
```

**Object anchor:** Her hand on the duvet — mirrors his hand on chest

**Cost/obstacle:** She looks toward him, longer than he did — but she doesn't reach either. Not yet.

---

### Shot 3 — THE REACH (12s)

**Seedance 2.0 / Kling 3.0 Prompt:**
```
Continuing directly from previous shots, same warm bedroom, candlelight walls, night.
Wide shot of both of them in bed — phones down now, both screens dark.
Ceiling above them, the only light is the warm amber bedside lamp.
A beat of stillness. Both staring at the ceiling.
Then — his hand slowly moves across the space between them, fingers uncurling.
Her hand meets it halfway, fingers interlacing without looking.
Neither of them turns. They just stare at the ceiling together, hands held.
A slow pull-back — wider and wider — until they're small in the frame, the warm room large around them.
Camera: starts medium wide, slow pull back to full wide, then continues to extreme wide.
35mm film grain, warm amber, soft vignette, silence in the frame.
The last frame: two small people, warm light, one joined hand.
```

**Object anchor:** His hand (established Shot 1) + Her hand (established Shot 2) = joined hands (resolution)

**Cost resolved:** The distance WAS the cost. The reach IS the resolution.
