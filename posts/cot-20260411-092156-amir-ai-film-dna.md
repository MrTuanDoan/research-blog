## STEP 2: What are the specific prompt principles?

### Principle 1: Emotional specificity via sequential micro-actions (not adjectives)
❌ "A father looks confused"
✅ "Blank stare, slow blink, sips coffee, looks up at ceiling, then back at the kid, shakes his head and walks away"

**Why it works:** Models understand actions, not emotions. 5 micro-actions > 1 adjective. Always.

### Principle 2: One impossible thing in one normal place
The contrast formula: EXTRAORDINARY SUBJECT + ORDINARY CONTEXT
- Piano on Mars
- Astronaut in a Moroccan souk
- 500-foot stone giant playing violin on a fjord
- Girl drawing galaxies with a yellow crayon

**Why it works:** The brain snags on incongruity. It's free to make. Same cost as a boring shot.

### Principle 3: Objects carry emotion better than faces
- Faces drift between generations (consistency problem in AI video)
- Objects stay locked
- Build stories around objects → visual continuity for free
- Red leather book. Pair of mugs. Broken crayon.

**Why it works:** Objects = stable identity anchors. Characters = unstable AI drift.

### Principle 4: Give the character a cost
Version 1: Girl drawing a universe (cool)
Version 2: Her crayon breaks and she keeps drawing with the broken piece (something else entirely)

**Formula:** Cool scene + obstacle/cost/problem = affecting scene. Every time.

### Principle 5: Chain shots forward (not batch)
Last frame of shot 1 → first frame of shot 2 → chain forward
Result: Visual continuity across a full film from individual generations

**Why it works:** Each shot has a "memory" of the previous one. Not isolated clips.

### Principle 6: Imperfection IS the style (VHS finding)
VHS 90s aesthetic: 85%+ success rate. Highest performer.
Grain, warmth, handheld shake, overexposed highlights = features not bugs.
**Insight:** When the style rewards imperfection, the model doesn't need to be perfect.

## STEP 3: What is the monitoring/agent system?

**Three agent types:**
1. **Trend Monitor** — watches X/niche 24/7, flags posts crossing 10K impressions in 4hrs + crossing multiple audiences, surfaces content angles
2. **Performance Tracker** — tracks published content, correlates visual styles → engagement → conversions
3. **Cross-referencing Agent** — generation data × engagement data = which styles convert (not just look good)

**Weekly Board Meeting prompt:**
- Round 1: Best + worst content this week, why
- Round 2: One format/topic gaining momentum + 3 specific examples
- Round 3: Cross-ref performance vs pipeline → accelerate/kill
- Round 4: Find ONE contradiction between stated strategy and actual data
- Round 5: Single highest-leverage action for next week — defended with numbers

**Key insight from board meeting:** Not three priorities. ONE. Defended with data. Forces clarity.
