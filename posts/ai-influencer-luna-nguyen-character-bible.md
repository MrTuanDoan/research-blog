```

marble bathroom vanity.

She holds a small glass serum dropper bottle, examines it for a moment,

No cuts. One long continuous video. Soft morning window light from left,
```

## 8. Notes

- Luna's power is **understatement** — she should never look like she's
  trying. All prompts should reinforce this: calm over excited, real over
  polished, knowing over enthusiastic.
- Freckles and beauty mark are identity anchors — always reference them
  explicitly in prompts to prevent drift.
- Morning light is her signature lighting — always warm, always from left.
- The jade gua sha is her signature prop alongside serums.
- Best performing tone for her niche: *quiet authority* — not tutorial,
  not hype, just "this is what works for me and you can tell it's working."
