## 4. THE DECISION

### 🏆 PRIMARY NICHE: **Niche B — AI Filmmaking / One-Person Studio**

**But not "AI tool reviews."** The angle is:

> **"One human. Two AI avatars. No crew. Full cinematic productions."**

Positioning: *The world's first AI film studio run by a couple.* Every piece of content is either:
1. A finished cinematic short (the product)
2. A BTS of how it was made (the education)
3. A hot take on the AI film world (the opinion)

This leverages:
- Niche B momentum (exploding, 3-6 month first-mover window)
- The couple avatar as a **unique visual identity** nobody else has
- Tuan's actual deep skills (Seedance, Kling, Veo, cinematic prompting, ai-influencer workflow)
- Highest viral ceiling (cinematic shorts can hit 10M+ organically)
- Builds directly toward the Skool "behind the scenes" community

### 🥈 SECONDARY LAYER: **Build-in-Public (Niche A)**

Not a separate niche — the *meta-content layer* on top of B. Every month, one long-form post/video: "Here's the AI brain behind the studio — how it evolved this month." This:
- Attracts other builders (Skool audience)
- Differentiates from pure filmmakers
- Creates B2B inbound (agencies, brands, tool companies want to sponsor the system-builder)

### Niche C: **NOT NOW.** Good monetization but wrong fit for the couple avatar + viral ceiling is lower. Can re-enter later as a sponsored breakdown series.
